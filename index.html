Questions

Good to know

Is E-Motion Chat free?

Yes. Sharing a Motion, chatting, safety tools and crisis support are free. E-Motion Plus is optional and adds meetups, unlimited calls, a wider map and more, with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan.

Is it anonymous?

Yes, by default. People see a nickname and your initials, never your real name. Your photo only shows if you choose to reveal it.

Can people see where I live?

No. Your location is rounded to about half a mile on your phone and again on our servers, so nobody sees your exact spot.

Is this therapy?

No. E-Motion Chat is peer support: kind people who've been through similar things. For ongoing support, a licensed therapist can help. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 in the US.

Who can join?

Adults 18 and older who agree to our Terms and Community Guidelines. There's zero tolerance for abuse, and accounts that break the rules are removed.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Me › Account › Delete account. You can also request deletion here.

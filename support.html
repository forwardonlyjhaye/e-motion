Support

Questions, bugs, account help, data requests, or safety concerns: email emotionchatsupport@forwardonlyllc.com. We read every message and usually reply within 2 business days.

If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself: E-Motion Chat is peer support, not an emergency service. In the US, call or text 988. Elsewhere, find a free, confidential line at findahelpline.com. If you're in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

Report someone

In any chat, tap the shield, then Report. Reports are confidential. You can also block someone from their profile or the shield menu.

Delete your account

Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, Motion, photos, chats you're part of and ratings. If you can't access the app, email us from any address and tell us your nickname; we'll verify and delete it.

Subscriptions

E-Motion Plus is billed by Apple. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
